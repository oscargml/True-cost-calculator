What Is Opportunity Cost?

Opportunity cost is the value of what you give up when you choose one financial decision over another. When you spend money on subscriptions, streaming services, apps, or recurring purchases, you also give up the potential investment growth that money could generate over time.

For example, a $15 monthly subscription may not seem expensive today, but if invested consistently over 10 years with compound interest, it could grow into thousands of dollars.

Why Compound Interest Matters

Compound interest allows investments to grow exponentially over time. Small recurring expenses can have a surprisingly large long-term impact on wealth building.
