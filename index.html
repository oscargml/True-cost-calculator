What Is Opportunity Cost?

Opportunity cost is the value of what you give up when you spend money instead of investing it. A $15 monthly subscription may not seem expensive, but over 10 years that same money could grow significantly through compound interest.

The True Cost Calculator helps you understand the hidden long-term impact of recurring expenses like streaming services, gym memberships, apps, and subscriptions.

Why Small Monthly Expenses Matter

Many people focus on large purchases while ignoring recurring expenses. However, small monthly costs compound over time and can reduce future wealth creation.
